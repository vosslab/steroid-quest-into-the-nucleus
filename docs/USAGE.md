# Playing Steroid Quest

Choose **Start adventure**, then follow the current action above the game world. Complete the
required encounters in each region and enter its biological destination. Reading and fragments
are optional; the game retains progress only while this page stays open.

## Touch controls

On a device with a coarse pointer, play in landscape. Drag the direction pad from your initial
touch position to steer left, right, up, or down. Moving within 12 CSS pixels leaves that axis
neutral. The separate **Pulse** button gives an upward pulse on a tap; holding it gives continuous
upward thrust. Use both controls together. Tap Pulse to escape a ride or sticky attachment, or
when the transcription marker enters the bright zone to recruit machinery.

Controls sit beside the complete game world. The current action, progress and Pause stay above
it. The mobile landscape view fits the dynamic viewport and device safe areas.

Rotating to portrait pauses active play and clears held input. Rotate back and tap **Resume** to
continue. Menus remain usable in portrait. Pause offers **Retry checkpoint**; the ending offers
**Replay**. Leaving the page or moving focus away from the canvas pauses play.

## Keyboard controls

Left and Right steer; tap Space to pulse upward, or hold it to rise. Optional Up and Down give
gentle continuous adjustment. Escape pauses and resumes; R retries the checkpoint. Menus use
Tab and Enter. Keyboard and touch holds compose independently on hybrid devices.

Retry preserves checkpoints, collected fragments, milestones and opened routes. Replay resets
the campaign. Sound defaults to on and can be toggled on the title, HUD and pause menu.
